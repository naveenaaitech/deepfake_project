ETHICAL FORENSIC PROTOCOL

Responsible AI & Verification Principles

Automated detection can make mistakes. Deepfake detection and audio spoof classifiers operate on statistical likelihoods, pattern heuristics, and probabilistic neural networks. They are susceptible to both false positives (labeling authentic media as synthetic) and false negatives (failing to identify sophisticated manipulations).

Environmental factors—such as re-encoding artifacts, low-light video capture, aggressive audio compression (e.g., WhatsApp or social media recompression), and non-native accents—can degrade detector accuracy.

Crucial Guidance: Results generated by SPECULUM or any AI forensic tool should never be treated as definitive legal proof or indisputable truth. Always combine automated indicators with human editorial scrutiny, source provenance tracking, metadata verification, and multi-source corroboration before publishing or drawing critical conclusions.
